Install Mobile / Desktop App

Install Wealth Plus on this Device

Install Wealth Plus on your phone or desktop home screen to use it full-screen without the browser URL bar, with fast offline loading and instant login.

How to Install Manually from Browser:
Android (Chrome / Brave / Edge)
  1. Tap the 3 vertical dots menu (⋮) at the top-right corner of Chrome.
  2. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Tap "Install" to confirm. Wealth Plus will appear on your phone home screen!
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share icon () at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" ().
  3. Tap "Add" at top-right. Wealth Plus icon will be added!
